# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The short version is below. The linked text is the full code.

## Our pledge
We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Expected behaviour
- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to multiboxing or to open source.
- Respect different opinions, experiences and setups.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Not acceptable
- Sexualised language or imagery, or sexual attention of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information (including account or character names) without their permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement
This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project
elsewhere. Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately: use GitHub's **Report content** on the
comment or issue, or open a private report under
[Security → Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/WoW-Multiboxing/wowboxer-layouts/security/advisories/new)
with "Code of Conduct" in the title. Every report is reviewed and kept confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove
contributions and warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, following the
Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
